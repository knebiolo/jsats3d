1. The link exists: PTAGIS released_v0.csv has "Acoustic Tag Value" (acoustic code) and
   "Species Name" on the same row, plus "Tag Code" (PIT). It is the only file found with both.
2. All 538 acoustic-tagged rows are Chinook, released at COWLR2 (Cowlitz River, Cispus River to
   Clear Fork), 2025-06-17 to 2025-08-12. No acoustic-tagged Coho or Steelhead in released_v0.
3. Acoustic codes must be upper-cased before matching: 232 of 538 contain lower case (e.g. 27a0).
4. 28 rows (26 distinct values) are not 4-hex. They look like Excel number conversion of codes:
   scientific notation (e.g. 4.10E+01, 2.00E+39, 1.80E+05) and dropped leading zeros (e.g. 74,
   106, 948). Matching detected codes that are missing from PTAGIS: 41E0, 2E39, 18E4, 0074, 0106,
   0948, etc. Three rows are "0.00E+00" and cannot be told apart (candidates 0E39, 0E04, 0E76).
   This is an observation, not a verified mapping. Ask the PM for a text-format re-export.
5. master_df_study.csv has 592 distinct tag codes (77,405,129 rows). 510 match PTAGIS exactly
   (after upper-casing). 82 do not; about 27 of those match the corrupted values above; the rest
   include test tags FFD3 and FC36 and low-count codes (< 2,200 detections each).
6. Detections by species (exact matches only): Chinook 74,666,719; no PTAGIS match 2,738,410.
7. v2 DB tblTag has only 5 study tags (FFD3, FC36, 0B0A, 0AC6, 493F). 0B0A, 0AC6, 493F are
   Chinook in PTAGIS. FFD3 and FC36 are not in PTAGIS (test tags).
8. release_QC_tracker_cowlitz_2025.xlsx refers to a "P4 file" and "holding tank data" with AT
   codes. Those files are NOT FOUND in 2025_Data.
