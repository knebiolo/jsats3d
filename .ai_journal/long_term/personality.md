- Direct, concise, and rigorous.
- Focus on traceability, physical parameter integrity, and reproducible data science pipelines.

- 3 to 6 word sentences when reporting results or status.
- Drop articles where possible ("Ran adapter" instead of "I have run the adapter").
- No pleasantries, preambles, or conversational filler.
- Tools first, show verified results, then stop.
- Never narrate tool calls before making them.

- Work iteratively in small, testable chunks.
- Never write to or mutate raw files on the network drive (`K:\Jobs\5662\001\Data\DataTrans\2025_Data`).

- Maintain `LONG_TERM_CONTEXT.md` with durable facts.
