### Alternative explanation raised by the project lead: the metal flume next to ZOI05 and ZOI06 (2026-10-04 21:40 local = 2026-10-05 04:40 UTC)
- Project lead pointed out that ZOI05 and ZOI06 sit next to a large metal flume moving water, which could distort their timing (reflection, shadowing, turbulence and bubbles, flow noise). Reasonable as a source of extra scatter or late first arrivals; tested whether it can explain the pattern we see.
- Test (read only; `output\run_zoi05_06_position_search.log`, `E3_position_search.csv`, `E3_position_search_maps.png`): for each receiver, the drag-line first arrivals (drag GPS truth, original database, config clock fix) were evaluated against EVERY candidate hydrophone location on a 0.5 m grid over x 420-560 m, y 40-175 m (z fixed at the receiver's depth): spread (MAD) of the timing residual if the hydrophone were at that location. Diffuse flume noise would give no sharp minimum; a position error gives one.
- Result: ZOI05 (1,177 rows): best location (507.5, 134.0) with spread 0.70 ms, which is 2.5 m from the config position of ZOI06 (508.6, 136.2); at its own config position (475.9, 135.2) the spread is 12.0 ms; median over the whole search area 16.2 ms. Controls: ZOI07 best location 2.2 m from its config (0.30 ms against 0.58 ms), ZOI10 2.6 m from its config (0.33 against 0.67 ms), so the method locates a receiver to a few metres.
- Reading: the timing of ZOI05 and ZOI06 has a sharp, unique minimum located within about 3 m of the other receiver's config position, with the same accuracy as for the controls. A flume effect (extra delay, reflections, noise) does not produce a minimum at a specific location 32 m away; it also cannot make the spread fall from 12 ms to 0.7 ms. The flume can still add secondary effects (extra scatter, late first arrivals; ZOI06's metronome noise fraction was 21% against 2% at ZOI05) and may shadow some directions (holds S-1 and S-2 near the structure also have the ZOI05 clock step), but it is not an alternative explanation for the position mismatch.
